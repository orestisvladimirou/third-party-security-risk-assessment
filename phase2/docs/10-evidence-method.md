# Phase 2 — Evidence evaluation method

**Scenario date: 2026-11-19 (fictional future scenario, not a real assessment).**

Record evidence **origin, coverage, completeness, freshness, ability to corroborate,** and **limits**. A supplier assertion is not proof. Absence of evidence does not prove a control is missing; it means the reviewer cannot substantiate its effectiveness.

All “verified” results exist **only inside the designed training simulation**. They are not real technical samples, genuine external assurance, actual contract signatures, or legal opinions.

**Numerical risk reduction** requires a credible control mechanism tied to likelihood or impact. The 1–5 scales are subjective expert judgments, not statistical probabilities. Controls planned but not implemented are not credited. Business approval also requires independent legal, privacy and operational gates, as appropriate.

For a real financial entity, DORA scope and whether an ICT service supports a critical or important function must be determined explicitly; Article 30 has baseline contract requirements and enhanced requirements for relevant functions. GDPR Article 28 applies to controller–processor arrangements. Simulated contract checks do not authorize real processing.
