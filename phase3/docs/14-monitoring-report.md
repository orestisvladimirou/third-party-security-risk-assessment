# 14 — Simulated monitoring escalation report

**Entirely fictional portfolio exercise.** Scenario date: 2026-12-01.

A payroll supplier proposes adding an overseas subprocessor, but the change notice lacks a complete data-flow and authorization record. There is no allegation of an actual transfer, incident, breach, or legal violation.

Earlier scoped supplier-access checks did not cover this new proposed path. R-003 is re-evaluated from **2×4=8 Medium** to **3×4=12 High**; impact remains unchanged. Payroll fallback risk R-004 remains **3×2=6 Medium**.

**Decision:** The model risk committee withdraws its earlier fictional conditional approval and returns payroll to **HOLD**. The subprocessor and contractual review is reopened separately from numerical risk scoring.

**Action ACT-007:** Obtain complete processing scope, location, authorization and technical evidence, and reassess prior to any further scenario approval. No real supplier is approved or data processed.

Cloud and support vendors remain HOLD. This case illustrates why supplier monitoring continues after a conditional onboarding decision.
