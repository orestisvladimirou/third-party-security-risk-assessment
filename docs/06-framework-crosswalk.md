# 06 — Framework crosswalk and primary sources

This is a **conceptual mapping**, not a statement of official conformity. ISO/IEC content is summarized at the control-title level; proprietary standard text is not reproduced.

| Deliverable | NIST CSF 2.0 outcome | Related ISO/IEC 27001:2022 concepts | Regulatory consideration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scope, policy, owners | GV.SC-01, GV.SC-02 | ISMS governance, responsibilities, supplier relationships (Annex A 5.19) | DORA Art. 28 where applicable |
| Vendor inventory, tiering | GV.SC-04 | Supplier relationship evaluation (A.5.19) | DORA Art. 28 criticality and register of arrangements |
| Questionnaires, evidence review | GV.SC-06, GV.SC-07 | ICT supply-chain risk (A.5.21), cloud service security (A.5.23) | DORA Art. 28(4) diligence |
| Risk register, treatment | GV.SC-03, GV.SC-07 | ISMS risk assessment and treatment | DORA Art. 28 third-party risk |
| Security clauses and DPA | GV.SC-05 | Supplier agreement requirements (A.5.20) | DORA Art. 30; GDPR Art. 28 if processor |
| Incident coordination | GV.SC-08 | Supplier monitoring/change (A.5.22) | DORA Art. 30 assistance where applicable |
| Ongoing monitoring | GV.SC-07, GV.SC-09 | Monitoring/review supplier service (A.5.22) | DORA Art. 28 oversight |
| Exit, data return and deletion | GV.SC-10 | Cloud acquisition/use/exit (A.5.23) | DORA Arts. 28–30; GDPR Art. 28(3) |
| Concentration risk | GV.SC-03, GV.SC-04, GV.SC-07 | ISMS risk analysis / treatment | DORA Art. 29 for critical/important dependencies |

## Official reference sources (checked 5 November 2026)

1. NIST, **SP 1305, CSF 2.0: Quick-Start Guide for C-SCRM**: https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/1305/final
2. NIST, **Cybersecurity Framework 2.0**: https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework
3. ISO, **ISO/IEC 27001:2022 overview**: https://www.iso.org/standard/27001
4. EUR-Lex, **Regulation (EU) 2022/2554, DORA**, notably Articles 28–30: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2022/2554/oj
5. EUR-Lex, **Regulation (EU) 2016/679, GDPR**, notably Article 28: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj
6. European Commission, **GDPR application to controller/processor relationships**: https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/information-business-and-organisations/application-gdpr_en

DORA and GDPR can be supplemented by delegated or implementing acts, supervisory expectations and applicable national rules. A genuine engagement requires identifying the entity type, competent authorities, contractual services, processing roles, and current applicable texts. This simulation is deliberately narrower than a legal compliance assessment.
