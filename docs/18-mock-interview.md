# Supplier Risk Interview Practice

This is a fictional training scenario, not a live supplier assessment.

A prospective SaaS supplier claims to use MFA, but has not provided scope-specific evidence. Explain what evidence you would request, how you would prioritize the risks, and which business approvals would be required before onboarding.

Practice giving a one-minute assessment and a clear management recommendation. Use the methodology and examples elsewhere in this repository to check your reasoning.
