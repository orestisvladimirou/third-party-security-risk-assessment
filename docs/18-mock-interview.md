# Supplier Risk Interview Practice

This is a fictional training scenario, not a live supplier assessment.

A prospective SaaS supplier claims to use MFA, but has not provided scope-specific evidence. Explain what evidence you would request, how you would prioritize the risks, and which business approvals would be required before onboarding.

Practice giving a one-minute assessment and a clear management recommendation. Use the methodology and examples elsewhere in this repository to check your reasoning.

## Interview questions

1. Which data and business functions are exposed, and how would you determine supplier criticality?
2. What is the difference between a security assertion and evidence supporting a residual risk reduction?
3. If you provisionally assign likelihood 3 and impact 4 to unauthorized access, what does the score mean?
4. Who should approve the contractual and privacy conditions?
5. What would justify reconsidering a HOLD decision?

## Example decision

A provisional **3 × 4 = 12 High** is reasonable if the scenario suggests meaningful customer-data exposure and uncertain preventive controls. The out-of-scope documentation does not establish that the relevant access control is operating. Request scoped evidence and keep onboarding on HOLD pending the organization's authorized risk and contract review.

A risk score is an ordinal prioritization judgment, not a measured probability. More than one score may be reasonable if the assumptions are documented.

## Self-assessment

Give yourself up to two points each for: business scoping, distinct risk events, evidence evaluation, decision governance, and clear communication. Aim for **8/10** before using this as an interview example.
